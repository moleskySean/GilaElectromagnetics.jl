Physical Units

Gila works in wavelengths and stores everything divided by $Z_\circ$, so its numbers carry no units. This page shows how to get real units in and out with Unitful.jl. Gila does not depend on Unitful, so the conversions are done by hand at the boundary.

Currents in

<!– Unitful.Z0 .* fld.dat .* u"A*s/m" gives V s m⁻¹. The one step people forget. Same for G * p and for the scattered field from a solve. –>
